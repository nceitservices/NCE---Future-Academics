Our Schools

Our schools form inclusive communities that foster each child’s academic and personal development with the application of a progressive, dynamic and innovative educational model.Our principals are experienced Emirati educational leaders who have worked within their communities for many years.

Principals in our schools have decades of leadership experience. It’s crucial that each community understands the vision, mission and values of the school and the characteristics of our schools and what they are able to offer to the community. Our students’ safety is our first and most important priority, and in addition to this, our school leaders manage daily activities, coordinate curricula, and oversee teachers and other school staff to provide a safe and productive learning environment for students.

Our Principals are deeply committed to ensuring their schools nurture and develop, not only outstanding academic achievement, but a strong sense of self, resilience, compassion, a deep appreciation of the rich diversity of our society and a commitment to be of service to the local community and the larger world in which we live.

Al Mutanabi School

At Al Mutanabi Charter School, we strive to educate all students to the highest level of academic achievement. We enable them to reach and expand their potential, preparing them to become productive, responsible, ethical, creative, and compassionate members of society. Our highly trained staff engage in ongoing continuous professional development and we partner with parents to help our students and our community to be their best.

Our Academic approach

At Al Mutanabi Charter School, our teaching program combines best practices with flexibility around how material is taught and assessed. It offers room for exploration and builds stronger teacher-student relationships, while prioritizing creativity. Our approach provides students with a one-of-a-kind experience where they have the opportunity to choose certain subjects to study.  We offer a strong foundation with rigorous standards for completing each grade. At the same time, teachers collaborate to create units of work that are centered around students, in ways that encourage them to think out of the box for solutions.

Our Facilities

At Al Mutanabi Charter School, we are proud of our state-of-the-art facilities, which include a robotics laboratory, as well as swimming and performance areas. Our facilities support our children to learn, develop and grow not only their knowledge, but also a set of skills they can take into their working lives to become the UAE’s managers and leaders of tomorrow.
